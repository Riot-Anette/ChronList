Privacy & your data

Guest tasks

Guest tasks are stored in this browser. Anyone using the same browser profile may access them. Clearing browser data can erase them.

Account and cloud data

When you sign in, your task names, notes, due dates, priorities, and completion details are saved in AWS in Europe (Ireland). Amazon Cognito handles your email, password, and account verification. ChronList does not store your password in its task database.

Signed-in tasks stay separate from the guest list. Guest tasks are copied into your account only when you choose “Copy guest tasks to my account”. The browser originals remain.

Sign-in sessions

Access tokens are kept in this tab’s session storage and removed when you sign out or the app processes session expiry. ChronList does not retain refresh tokens. You may need to sign in again when your session expires.

Reminders

Reminders require ChronList to remain open and your device to stay awake. Sound must be enabled again after a page reload. Browser and device restrictions can delay or prevent reminders.

Notifications may display task names on your device, including its lock screen, depending on your settings. You can manage notification permissions in your browser.

Service providers

ChronList uses Netlify for hosting, AWS for authentication and cloud tasks, and Google Fonts for typography. These providers receive the network information needed to deliver their services.

Backups

Use Export backup to download your tasks. Backups contain task data, not authentication tokens. Keep downloaded files private, especially if your tasks contain personal information.

Restoring a backup adds copies of its tasks to the selected account or guest list. It does not replace existing tasks.

Task retention and deletion

Cloud tasks remain stored until you delete them or request account deletion. Deleting a cloud task removes its active database record. Recovery backups may retain deleted records until the configured AWS recovery window expires.

Guest copies and downloaded backups are separate from cloud data and must be deleted separately.

Contact and account deletion
